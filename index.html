    Common Questions

    Can you help if my visa was rejected before?

    Yes. We rebook your appointment, prepare you 1-on-1, and help you apply again with a stronger case.

    Do I have to fly to the US to open a company?

    No. You can open your American company from Saudi Arabia, and we handle all the registration forms for you.

    Can I apply from Saudi Arabia if I am not a Saudi citizen?

    Usually, yes. Applicants normally apply from where they legally live, so a valid Iqama or Premium Residency lets you apply from within the Kingdom. We confirm the requirements for your passport before you start.

    Is my information kept private?

    Yes. Your business and family details stay completely confidential.

    Can you guarantee my visa will be approved?

    No one can. Every visa decision belongs to the government of the country you are applying to. Our job is to make your application complete and prepare you well.
