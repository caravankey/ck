Advisory Clarifications

Frequently Addressed Protocols

Can all expatriate nationalities in Saudi Arabia apply for Western visas from the Kingdom?

Yes. Western consulates (Schengen, UK, US, and Canada) explicitly expect foreign nationals to apply from their country of lawful, primary residence. Holding a valid Saudi Iqama or Premium Residency satisfies the jurisdiction requirement to lodge your application in Riyadh, Jeddah, or Dhahran.

How does Caravan & Key secure earlier embassy appointment slots?

Embassies release appointment batches and cancellations continuously throughout the business day. Our concierge team actively monitors slots across the US Embassy in Riyadh, the US Consulate in Dhahran, and European VFS/TLS centers to reschedule families into early windows before school holiday departures.

What accommodation types are included in Caravan & Key packages?

We specialize in family residences: 2-to-4 bedroom luxury serviced apartments, private townhomes, and resort villas. All stays are handpicked in prime, safe locations with nearby dining and transit, and include embassy-approved cancellation terms.

How do we prepare for the US Embassy consular interview?

Prior to your interview in Riyadh, Dhahran, or Jeddah, our desk conducts a 1-on-1 private simulation briefing. We coach you on articulately stating your vacation itinerary, presenting your Chamber-attested files, and confidently answering inquiries regarding your career in the Kingdom.
